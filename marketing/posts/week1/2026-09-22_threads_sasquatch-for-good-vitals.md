Sasquatch for Good is the part of what we build that isn't trying to make money.

Squatch Vitals is a simple daily record: weight, blood pressure, oxygen, sleep, the numbers a doctor asks you to track. It can even photograph a handwritten paper log and read the numbers off the page, so months of tracking don't have to be retyped by hand.

No account. No server. No ads. Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you choose to send a report.

Free, because it should be. Coming soon to the App Store.
