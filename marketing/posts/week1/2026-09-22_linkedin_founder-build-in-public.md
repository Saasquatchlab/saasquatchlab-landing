A small number I think about a lot: SizeSquatch gives you three free exports before asking for $4.99.

That number wasn't a funnel we A/B tested. Three exports is roughly how many times I resized something for myself before I trusted the crop tool not to do something dumb: an off-center crop, a stretched video, a passport photo with the wrong margins. If it takes three tries for me to trust my own app, three free tries seemed like a fair thing to hand a stranger too.

The lesson I keep relearning building these apps solo: pricing and trust are the same decision. A subscription on a tool this small would have made the trust question irrelevant to me and very relevant to everyone else, every month, forever.

One-time unlock. No subscription. iOS and Android.
