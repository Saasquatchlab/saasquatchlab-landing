Passport photos are a small, annoying tax on travel. Wrong size, wrong background, retake at the drugstore.

Here's the 20 second version in SizeSquatch: open the app, pick the US 2x2 inch or UK/EU passport preset, drag your photo into the frame (it's clamped so you can't accidentally crop out your own ears), export. The sizing rules are built into the preset, so you're not guessing at inches or DPI.

It's the same editor used for Instagram, LinkedIn, and ad sizes, 45 presets in total, just pointed at a harder problem than a social media crop.

Three exports free, then $4.99 once, no subscription. iOS and Android.

Try it before your next trip.
