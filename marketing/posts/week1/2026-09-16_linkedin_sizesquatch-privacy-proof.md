Every resize app asks the same implicit question: where do my photos go while this runs?

SizeSquatch's answer is the boring one: nowhere. No upload, no cloud render, no server of ours to send anything to. Cropping, resizing, even re-encoding video for Reels or TikTok, all happens on your phone using the hardware you already own.

We built it this way for a simple reason. A passport photo, a kid's school picture, a screenshot you don't want indexed somewhere: none of that needs to leave your device to get resized correctly.

Three free exports, then a one-time $4.99 unlock. No subscription, no account, no photo library scraping, just the system photo picker so it only ever touches what you hand it.

Available on iOS and Android.
