A separate promise

Sasquatch for Good

Sasquatch for Good is a small, separate family of apps built to help, not to make money. Every app in it is free, asks for no account, carries no ads, and sells no data — and there is no upsell waiting once you are in, because that was never the plan.

Everything else about how we build — privacy-first design, no ad trackers, no data brokers, no dark patterns — already applied to these apps before this family had a name. What sets them apart is the reason they exist: someone needed a tool that did not exist yet, or existed only behind a paywall, and building it was the whole point.

If you or someone you know could use one of these, or you have an idea for a tool that belongs in this family, we would like to hear it. Email hello@saasquatchlab.com.
