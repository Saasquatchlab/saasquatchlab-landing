SizeSquatch Privacy Policy

Effective August 31, 2026

The short version: SizeSquatch collects no data. None. Your photos are processed entirely on your device and never leave it.

What we collect

Nothing. SizeSquatch has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network connections of its own.

Your photos

All resizing, cropping, grid building, and file encoding happens locally on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's on-device frameworks. SizeSquatch uses Apple's photo picker, which means the app can only ever access the specific photos you choose — never your whole library. Exported images are saved only where you send them: your photo library or the share destination you pick.

Purchases

The optional "Unlock Everything" purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple provides us with anonymous, aggregate sales reports only. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Settings

Your preferences (default size, format, quality, remaining free exports) are stored on your device only and are removed if you delete the app.

Children

SizeSquatch does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Because the app collects nothing, we expect it to stay boring.

Contact

Questions? Email hello@saasquatchlab.com. SizeSquatch is made by SaaSquatch Lab.
